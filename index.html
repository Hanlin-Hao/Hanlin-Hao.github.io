About

I am a Master's student in Mathematics at the University of Bonn. My current interests lie broadly in algebraic geometry and representation theory, with particular interest in Hall algebras, moduli spaces, K-theory, and interactions with complex and symplectic geometry.

Research Interests

  • Algebraic geometry and moduli spaces
  • Hall algebras and K-theoretic Hall algebras
  • Representation theory
  • Complex and symplectic geometry
  • Homological mirror symmetry

K-theoretic Hall algebras of torsion sheaves on curves

Current Master's research project studying the K-theory of stacks of torsion sheaves on smooth projective curves and the associated Hall algebra structures.
